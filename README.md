# Dermatology Collective Clinic & Surgery — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Dermatology Collective Clinic & Surgery is a Singapore specialist skin clinic serving children and adults from its Orchard Road and Farrer Park locations. Its website introduces the clinic and its medical, paediatric, procedural and surgical, and cosmetic dermatology services, as well as allergy testing and skin cancer screening and management.

The redesigned homepage is focused on making the clinic's message easier to read, helping visitors contact the clinic quickly, and giving them clearer routes to explore its care services.

## Main Improvements

1. **Improved hero readability** — The previous hero placed text over a photo, making it difficult to read. The redesign moves the headline and supporting copy onto a solid cream background for clearer contrast and legibility.
2. **More accessible contact actions** — Phone and WhatsApp details previously appeared as plain text. They are now presented as prominent, tappable buttons, with both actions also available in a persistent mobile contact bar.
3. **Clearer service navigation** — The previous homepage offered few ways to explore the clinic's services. The redesign introduces six care pathways, each with a short description and a direct link. They are arranged in three columns on desktop and two columns on mobile.

## Design Improvements

- **Typography and contrast:** The cream hero text panel separates key messaging from the background photo and improves readability.
- **Layout and responsiveness:** The six care pathways use a clear grid that adapts from three columns on desktop to two on mobile.
- **Contact experience:** Prominent phone and WhatsApp buttons make the main contact actions easier to identify and use; the persistent mobile bar keeps them visible while browsing.
- **Content hierarchy:** The hero message, contact actions, and service pathways give the homepage clearer, more direct ways for visitors to understand the clinic and continue to relevant information.
